Common questions

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Open Settings → Account and sign in with Google on both phones. Coins, boosters and levels are merged, nothing is counted twice.

I bought something but did not get it.

Make sure you are online and reopen the game; an unfinished purchase is delivered automatically. You can also use Settings → Restore purchases. If Google Play shows the purchase but the game still does not, email us with your Player ID and the order number (GPA.…) from your Google Play receipt.

How do lives work?

A run that stops short costs one life. Lives refill over time; you can also refill them with coins or get one by watching an ad.

How do I turn off personalised ads or statistics?

Settings → Privacy. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete your account.
