Delete your account and data

Domino Reveal by NovaCode Dynamics · Hesabını ve verilerini sil

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Domino Reveal and tap the gear icon (Settings).
  2. Tap Delete my account and data (signed in) or Delete my data (guest).
  3. Confirm. Your server copy is deleted at once, then the data on the phone.

Without the game (by email)

Email info@novacodedynamics.com with the subject “Domino Reveal account deletion” and your Player ID (Settings → Account). If you no longer have the game, tell us the Google account you signed in with and the approximate date you last played, and we will help identify the account. We handle requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account link (Google or Apple ID), cloud backup, progress, coins, boosters and settings stored on our server.
  • Gameplay statistics linked to your installations.
  • For Sign in with Apple, we also revoke the game’s access to your Apple ID.

What is kept

  • Purchase records (product, store token, date) are kept for refunds, fraud prevention and accounting, without any link to you.
  • Totals that cannot identify anyone (for example “how many players finished level 20”).

Uninstalling the game alone does not delete the server copy; use one of the ways above.
