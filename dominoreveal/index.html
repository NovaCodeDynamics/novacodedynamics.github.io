Domino Reveal

A calm domino puzzle. Fill the gaps with straight and corner pieces, tap the start domino, and watch the wave topple every tile to reveal the hidden picture.

Get it on Google PlaySupport

How to play

  • Islands of dominoes are joined by empty channels. Put a straight or corner piece in each gap.
  • Tap a placed piece to turn it; undo or clear with the buttons above the board.
  • Tap the start domino. If the chain reaches every island, the picture appears.
  • Hints, an extra piece and a preview run help on harder boards. Levels never run out.

Free to play with optional ads and in-app purchases. Available in 10 languages.
