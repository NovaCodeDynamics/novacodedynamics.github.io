Privacy Policy

Domino Reveal · effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the Domino Reveal game (“the game”) handles and why. The game is published by NovaCode Dynamics (“we”, “us”). Questions and privacy requests: info@novacodedynamics.com.

1. Information the game handles

WhatWhy
Random app identifiers created on your phone (a backup ID and a statistics ID)To back up your progress on our server without an account and to count gameplay statistics. They are not your name, phone number or device serial.
Account identifier if you choose “Sign in with Google” (or Apple on iPhone)To keep the same progress on all your phones. We store only the account’s unique ID that Google or Apple gives us. We do not ask for or store your name, email address or photo.
Game progress: levels reached, coins, boosters, daily reward streak, settingsTo save your game, restore it, and sync it between phones.
Purchase records: product, purchase token, verification resultTo check each purchase with Google Play, credit it once, and restore it. Payment details stay with Google; we never see card numbers.
Gameplay statistics: app version, platform, language, level starts and finishes, time on a level, use of hints, ads watched, shop visitsTo find levels that are too hard and keep the game working well. You can turn this off in Settings → Gameplay statistics.
Advertising data: the device advertising ID, consent choices, ad requests and interactions (handled by the ad providers listed below)To show ads, measure them and limit how often they appear. Personalised ads are used only where allowed and, where required, with your consent. You can turn them off in Settings → Personalised ads.
Emails you send usTo answer support and privacy requests.

The game does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files. Reminder notifications (lives refilled, daily reward) are scheduled on your phone and are off unless you turn them on.

2. Who processes it

  • Google: Google Play (distribution, billing and purchase verification), Google Sign-In, Google AdMob and the Google consent form (User Messaging Platform).
  • Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation) and the ad networks it uses to fill ads.
  • Railway: hosting of our game server and database (accounts, backups, purchase checks, statistics).
  • Apple: App Store, billing and Sign in with Apple, if the game is offered on iPhone.

Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect players and the service.

3. Legal bases

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on: performing our agreement with you (saving your game, accounts, purchases); our legitimate interest in keeping the game secure and improving it (statistics you can switch off); your consent for personalised advertising where consent is required; and legal obligations (for example keeping purchase records).

4. How long we keep it

  • Progress and backups: while you use the game. They are deleted when you delete your data or account.
  • Gameplay statistics: at most 24 months, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: kept as long as needed for refunds, fraud prevention and accounting, without a link to a deleted account.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to handle your request.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Play without signing in. Your progress is still backed up for that phone.
  • Turn off gameplay statistics and personalised ads in Settings; change the ad consent choice where the game offers Ad privacy options.
  • Delete everything from the game: Settings → Delete my data (or Delete my account and data when signed in). See how to delete your account, including by email.
  • Depending on where you live you may ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to processing. Email us with your Player ID (Settings → Account). You may also complain to your data protection authority.

6. Security and transfers

Connections to our server are encrypted (HTTPS) and the server only accepts sign-ins that Google or Apple confirm. Our providers may process data in countries other than yours; where required, they use recognised safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell players in the game.
