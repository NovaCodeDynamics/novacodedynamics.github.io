Useful details

Include your device, OS version, app version, and level number so we can investigate faster.

How the puzzle works

  1. Swipe a block in one of four directions. It slides until a wall, obstacle, or another block stops it.
  2. Guide each block through a gate of the same color. A numbered gate accepts exactly that size; ∞ accepts any size.
  3. A Rotate, Split, Stretch, or Shrink tile activates only when the block finishes its move on that tile.

I am stuck on a level

Tap Help in the game. It checks the board in its current state and shows one safe directional move. It does not reveal the full solution. You can also use Undo to revisit your last decision.

Restore a purchase

Open the in-game Store and choose Restore Purchases. Permanent purchases such as No Ads are tied to your Apple or Google store account. Consumable coin packs cannot be restored after they have been consumed.

Progress or account problem

Morph Jam can be played as a guest. If you linked a supported sign-in provider, use the same provider to recover synced progress. If the wrong progress appears, stop playing on the affected device and contact us before deleting data.

Advertising and privacy choices

You can review or change available advertising consent choices from Settings → Ad Choices. On iOS, tracking permission can also be changed in the system Settings app.

Report a bug

Email support@novacodedynamics.com. Please describe what you expected, what happened, and the last action you took. A screenshot is helpful, but never send passwords or payment-card information.
